My long-term interests are (in no particular order):
- Robotics, including physical AI, RL, manipulation.
- Continual learning, such as test-time training. 
- Efficiency gap: the human brain proves that "human-level intelligence" is possible with 20 watts of power, while trillion-parameter models require up to kilowatts. There could be a new architecture (or, a crucial reformation of LLMs) that will drastically reduce the power cost and make intelligence open to everyone.
- Native weight-based vs tool-based methodology, figuring out what combination of both makes the most sense for either solving a given problem, or for general intelligence.
